What you'll find when you get here

A small store that runs deep.

The cooler case

Frozen cuts of beef, pork, and chicken. Labeled, weighed, ready to grab. Whatever's in season, in stock that day.

The shelves

Raw honey, jam, salsa, pickles, dry goods, gift boxes. Everything bottled or jarred is small-batch, locally made.

The garden cooler

Whatever came out of the field that morning. Eggs in the fridge, herbs in jars of water, melons stacked on a wooden crate.
