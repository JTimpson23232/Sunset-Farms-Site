About our bacon

Most bacon is pumped with brine and run through a smokehouse in hours. Ours isn't. We cure every belly by hand with a custom brown sugar blend — organic pure cane sugar and organic blackstrap molasses — that builds a deep, rich sweetness from the inside out. Then we slow-smoke over hardwood hickory until it's done right. No liquid smoke. No compromises. This is craft bacon — developed here, cured here, smoked here. You won't find it anywhere else.

Honest answers

Questions, before you reserve.

How much freezer space do I need?
A half hog (est. ~78 lb packaged) fits in roughly 4 cubic feet of freezer space. A standard chest freezer (7 cu ft) holds a full half with room left over. A whole hog needs about 8 cu ft. We're happy to talk space planning before you reserve.
What if I've never bought a whole hog before?
Most of our customers haven't, the first time. We send you a link to your online cut sheet, then we get on a call to walk you through it. Ten minutes. You'll know exactly what you're ordering.
How does pricing work?
Pricing is based on actual hanging weight at processing. Whole hog: hog at $5.00/lb HW (est. ~$1,080) + kill fee $70 + cut/wrap/processing at $1.05/lb HW (est. ~$227) = est. total ~$1,377. Half hog: est. ~$688 total. $700 (whole) or $350 (half) holds your hog — deposit is non-refundable. Estimated balance is due at pickup. All amounts are estimates; final invoice adjusted to actual hanging weight.
