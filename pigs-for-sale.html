Why our pork tastes different

Hogs raised the way nature wrote it.

Most pork in America comes from hogs that never see daylight. Ours grow up the long way: open paddocks, deep straw bedding, dirt to root, water to wallow, and shade in the heat. They take a couple months longer to finish than industrial pork. The marbling tells you why we wait.

  • Berkshire and Duroc crosses. Heritage breeds selected for flavor, not factory speed.
  • Non-GMO grain ration, plus garden seconds, orchard windfalls, and dairy whey when we have it.
  • No hormones. No routine antibiotics. Sick animals are pulled and treated; they never reenter the food line.
  • Local custom processor. Small operation, USDA inspected, cuts to your written spec.

Honest answers

Questions, before you reserve.

How much freezer space do I need?
A half hog (about 80 lb) fits in roughly 4 cubic feet of freezer space. A standard chest freezer (7 cu ft) holds a full half with room left over. A whole hog needs about 8 cu ft. If you don't have a chest freezer, an upright works. We're happy to talk space planning before you reserve.
What if I've never bought a whole hog before?
Most of our customers haven't, the first time. We send a simple cut sheet, then we get on a call to walk you through it. Ten minutes. You'll know exactly what you're ordering.
Is the processing fee included in the price?
Yes. The price you see is the all-in price: hog, processing, custom cuts, smoking, sausage seasoning, vacuum sealing, and packaging. No surprise add-ons at the end.
How long does it take to get my hog?
From the day you reserve, plan on 3 to 4 weeks until delivery. Our processor batches once a week and we deliver every Wednesday and Saturday inside our zone.
What's the deposit policy?
$99 holds your hog. The deposit is fully refundable up until you finalize your cut sheet. After that, the hog is committed to processing and the deposit becomes non-refundable. The remaining balance is due before delivery.
Do you deliver outside Centennial Park?
Free delivery on Wednesdays to Centennial Park, Colorado City, Hildale, and Apple Valley. Free delivery on Saturdays to St. George, Hurricane, La Verkin, and Cedar City. Outside that zone, give us a call. We've worked things out before.
Can I split a half with a friend?
Absolutely. Lots of customers do quarter shares between two families. We can package the cuts in two batches at no extra charge. Just let us know on the cut sheet.
What if I'm not happy with it?
Open a package, taste it. If it's not the best pork you've eaten, call us within 14 days. We'll make it right. Refund or replacement, your call. We've never had to do it. We don't expect to.
