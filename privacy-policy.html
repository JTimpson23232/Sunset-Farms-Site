Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 1, 2025  |  Last updated: May 7, 2026

Sunset Farms Country Store operates the website at sunsetfarms.store. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Order and transaction data. When you place an order, we collect your name, shipping address, email, phone number, and payment details. Payment processing is handled by our third-party processor; we do not store full card numbers.

Contact and inquiry data. When you reach out by phone, email, or contact form, we collect the information you provide.

Newsletter and text opt-ins. If you subscribe to our email list or text updates, we collect your email address and/or phone number.

Farm visit reservations. If you schedule a visit or purchase event, we collect the name and contact details you submit.

Usage and log data. Our servers automatically record your IP address, browser type, referring URL, pages visited, and timestamps.

Cookies and analytics. We use cookies to remember preferences and analyze traffic. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill orders and communicate about them
  • To respond to inquiries and feedback
  • To send newsletters and promotions you have opted into
  • To improve our website, products, and services
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To detect and prevent fraud or misuse

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers who help us operate (payment processors, shipping carriers, email platforms, website hosts), when required by law, or in the event of a business transfer.

4. Email and Text Communications

You can unsubscribe from email at any time by clicking "unsubscribe" in any message, or from texts by replying STOP. We do not send marketing to people who have not opted in.

5. Data Retention

Order records are kept for as long as required for tax and legal purposes (typically seven years). Newsletter data is retained until you unsubscribe.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data. No method of internet transmission is 100% secure. Contact us immediately if you suspect a breach.

7. Children

Our site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information. Contact us if you believe we have done so and we will delete it promptly.

8. Third-Party Links

Our site links to third-party sites (our online store, social media, delivery partners) with their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold. Contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the updated date above. Continued use of the site after posting constitutes acceptance.
