Buy bigger, save more

Half & whole animals.

Our best price per pound is on the animal-share program. You reserve a quarter, half, or whole. We work the cut sheet with you. The processor cuts to your spec, wraps it, and freezes it. We deliver or you pick up.

  • Quarter beef. About 110 lb of cuts, perfect for a couple or small family.
  • Half beef. About 220 lb. The most common share.
  • Half hog. About 80 lb of bacon, chops, sausage, ham.
  • Whole hog. For canners, big families, freezer hawks.

Lead time runs 4 to 8 weeks depending on the season. We always have a few names on the list.

Common questions

Things people usually want to know.

Do you ship outside Arizona and Utah?
No. We're a local farm, not a shipping company. We deliver inside our weekly route only. If you're outside the route but close, call us. We've worked things out before.
How is the meat packaged?
All meat is vacuum-sealed, frozen, and labeled with the cut, weight, and packing date. Whole birds and roasts are frozen individually. Ground meats come in 1-lb packages by default; let us know if you want something different.
How long can I keep frozen meat?
Properly stored in a deep freezer at 0°F or below: ground meat 4 to 6 months, steaks and roasts 8 to 12 months, whole birds about 12 months, sausage about 3 months. We'd rather you eat it sooner than later.
What's your return policy?
If you ever open a package and it's not right, call us within 48 hours. We'll replace it or refund it. We stand behind everything that leaves the farm.
Can I subscribe?
No formal subscription program. We've found that people's freezers and weekly menus vary too much to make subscriptions a clean fit. We do keep a list of "regulars" we text when something they love comes back in stock.
Do you sell at farmers markets?
Occasionally, usually St. George and Hurricane in the warm months. Follow our Instagram @sunsetfarms.store for the latest stand schedule.
Can my kids visit the animals?
Please. Call ahead so we can make sure someone is around to walk you through. Tours are free, the kids can usually feed a chicken, and there's a barn cat or three to chase.
Do you take SNAP / EBT?
We do accept SNAP at the country store on eligible items. Online checkout doesn't currently support EBT. We're working on it.
