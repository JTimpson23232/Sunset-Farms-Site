A note from our family

We didn't set out to start a brand. We just wanted to feed our community well.

In 2005, we put a few cattle on a quiet stretch of grass south of the cliffs. We learned by the seasons. We made plenty of mistakes. The neighbors started asking for a roast, then a dozen eggs, then a hog for the freezer. Twenty years later the farm still runs at family scale. Every animal still has a name. And the door is still open.

Self-sufficiency is something we'll always be working toward, never something we'll finish. That's the part we love.

The Sunset Farms family

How we farm

Three things we won't
compromise on.

Twenty years of getting it wrong, getting it right, and writing down what worked. Here's what stays.

The animals come first.

Open air, shade, water, calm handling. No feedlots, no growth promoters, and no rushing the seasons. Healthy animals make better food. Full stop.

The land has to outlive us.

Compost from our own herd. Cover crops on the garden ground. We borrow this place from the next generation; we'd like to hand it back better than we got it.

You can come see for yourself.

The gate isn't locked. Call ahead, bring your kids, walk the farm. Transparency isn't a marketing word for us. It's how we'd want to buy our own food.
